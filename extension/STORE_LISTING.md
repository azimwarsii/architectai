## Extension Name
ArchitectAI - AI Coding Context Capture

## Short Description (132 characters max)
Capture context from AI coding tools like Cursor, Lovable, Bolt & Replit directly to your ArchitectAI product spec canvas.

## Detailed Description

**Bridge the gap between AI coding tools and your product specs**

ArchitectAI helps product managers and developers maintain alignment between AI-generated code and product requirements. Capture conversations, code snippets, and context from your favorite AI coding tools directly to your specification canvas.

**Supported Platforms:**
- Cursor
- Lovable
- Bolt.new
- Replit
- v0.dev

**Key Features:**

**Context Capture**
Select text, code, or AI conversations on any supported platform and instantly send them to your ArchitectAI canvas as evidence nodes. Keep track of implementation decisions and AI suggestions.

**Task Context**
View your active development tasks with acceptance criteria directly in the extension. Get AI-ready prompt suggestions based on your current task to maintain consistency across coding sessions.

**Prompt Logging**
Automatically log prompts and responses from AI coding tools to your project memory. Never lose track of important implementation decisions or AI guidance.

**Side Panel**
Use the persistent side panel to keep task context visible while you code. Copy full task context with one click to paste into your AI tool.

**How it works:**
1. Install the extension and sign in with your ArchitectAI account
2. Navigate to any supported AI coding platform
3. Select text or code you want to capture
4. Click the "ArchitectAI" button or use Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+A
5. Choose your project and node type in the popup
6. Your context is sent directly to your canvas

**Privacy & Security:**
- OAuth authentication with your ArchitectAI account
- Data is only sent to your own ArchitectAI projects
- No third-party tracking or analytics
- Open source codebase

Perfect for:
- Product managers tracking AI implementation decisions
- Developers maintaining spec-to-code traceability
- Teams coordinating across multiple AI coding tools
- Anyone who wants to document their AI-assisted development process
